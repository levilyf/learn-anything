# Evaluation plan

The first release is a teaching-loop prototype. Passing structural tests does not prove that the tutor teaches effectively.

## What to evaluate

- **Diagnosis:** Does the tutor identify prerequisite gaps without making the learner complete a long intake?
- **Clarity:** Can the learner explain the concept in their own words after the lesson?
- **Practice:** Can the learner solve a new, related task without copying the worked example?
- **Feedback:** Does feedback identify the actual misconception and lead to a better second attempt?
- **Adaptation:** Does the tutor change its explanation or task difficulty based on evidence?
- **Retention:** Can the learner retrieve and apply the concept in a later session?
- **Portability:** Can another compatible agent resume from the exported learner record?

## Minimum session record

For each evaluation session, record:
- Subject, learner starting level, and target outcome.
- Concepts taught and practice tasks attempted.
- Evidence of independent performance and support required.
- Misconceptions observed and how feedback changed the result.
- Follow-up retention check, when available.

Do not record unnecessary personal data. Obtain consent before collecting or publishing learner session data.

## Success criteria for the prototype

A session is promising when the learner can:
1. Explain the target concept accurately in their own words.
2. Apply it to a new but related problem.
3. Identify and correct a common mistake.
4. Resume the learning path from a portable progress summary.

These criteria are evaluation prompts, not a validated psychometric instrument. Gather multiple sessions and report limitations before making efficacy claims.

## What not to optimize

Do not use lesson count, response length, time spent, or quiz scores alone as proxies for learning. Avoid leaderboards and public learner profiles in the initial release.
