# Creating subject modules

Use [the subject module template](../subjects/_template/subject.md) and follow the [subject ecosystem blueprint](subject-ecosystem.md). The [subject catalogue](../subjects/catalog.md) is the index of planned and available areas.

## Authoring sequence

1. **Define scope:** choose a stable subject ID, audience, purpose, exclusions, version, and status.
2. **Map capability:** write observable outcomes and break the subject into domains, skill areas, concepts, and prerequisite links.
3. **Design routes:** define a core path and optional goal-oriented tracks. Use diagnostics to skip only what the learner can already demonstrate.
4. **Write teaching material:** explanations, worked examples, visual opportunities, misconceptions, practice, and alternate explanations.
5. **Assess capability:** create explanation, application, diagnosis, and transfer tasks with rubrics and answer guidance.
6. **Plan adaptation:** specify what to do when learners are stuck, partly correct, advanced, or returning after a gap.
7. **Review safety and sources:** check legal, privacy, physical, and domain-specific risks; verify changing claims against maintained sources.
8. **Test with learners:** observe where they misunderstand, fail, or need missing prerequisites. Revise before claiming validation.

## Required quality

A module should not be a list of topics or a mini textbook. It must guide the tutor's decisions. Every core concept needs an outcome, a clear explanation, a useful example, practice, misconception handling, and a way to assess independent understanding.

Use different assessment methods for different domains. A language learner needs communicative tasks; a mathematics learner needs reasoning and problem-solving; a business learner needs decisions and artifacts; a security learner needs evidence interpretation and safe practical work.

## Status labels

- **Listed:** catalogue entry only.
- **Outlined:** outcomes and dependency-aware roadmap exist.
- **Authored:** core teaching, practice, assessment, and adaptation material exists.
- **Reviewed:** subject-matter and safety review completed.
- **Validated:** representative learner tests demonstrate that the module works; gaps are documented.

Do not label a module Validated merely because its Markdown exists.

## Contribution review

Review scope, prerequisites, accuracy, accessibility, assessment quality, safety, sources, and maintenance burden. High-risk domains require an explicit safety review. Prefer small, coherent pull requests over a huge subject dump.
