# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in Learn Anything a respectful, welcoming, and constructive experience.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementation, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving learning outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement
This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project. Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for violations.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Include relevant links and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
