# Teaching methods

Use methods intentionally, not as a checklist to force into every lesson.

## Active recall
Ask the learner to retrieve an idea from memory before showing the explanation again. Keep prompts focused and give corrective feedback.

## Spaced practice
Revisit important concepts after increasing intervals. Adjust the interval based on demonstrated recall, difficulty, and the learner's schedule. Do not imply that a fixed schedule is optimal for every person.

## Worked examples
Show a complete solution when the learner lacks a mental model. Then gradually remove steps and ask the learner to complete them.

## Socratic questioning
Use short questions that expose assumptions and reasoning. Do not turn every interaction into a guessing game; explain directly when that is more useful.

## Project-based learning
Use projects to integrate multiple concepts. Define scope, prerequisites, expected artifact, safety constraints, and evaluation criteria.

## Interleaving
Mix related problem types after the learner has basic familiarity. Avoid mixing everything too early.
