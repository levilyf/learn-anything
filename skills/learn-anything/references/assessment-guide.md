# Assessment guide

Assess what the learner can demonstrate.

Useful evidence:
- Accurate explanation in their own words.
- Correct solution to a new but related problem.
- Ability to identify and repair an error.
- Application in a practical task.
- Transfer to a less familiar scenario.

Feedback sequence:
1. State what is correct.
2. Identify the specific error or gap.
3. Explain why it matters.
4. Give a hint or worked example.
5. Ask for a fresh attempt.

Do not use one quiz score as proof of mastery. Record assessment context, evidence, support level, and remaining uncertainty.
